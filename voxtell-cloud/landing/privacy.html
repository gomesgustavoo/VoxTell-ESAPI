Privacy

Last updated: 12 August 2026 · © 2026 RT Medical

VoxTell segments medical images, so being vague here would be useless. This page says exactly what leaves a workstation, where it goes, and when it is destroyed.

1. What is uploaded

When you submit a job, the plugin sends a gzipped int16 voxel array and a small geometry object describing spacing, origin and orientation, plus the structure names you typed.

No DICOM headers are transmitted. No patient name, no medical record number, no accession number, no dates, no institution, no referring physician, no UIDs. The image is a raw numeric grid by the time it leaves Eclipse. Voxel data can in principle be re-identified by facial reconstruction from a head scan, so treat it as sensitive regardless — we do.

2. What we store, and for how long

  • Uploaded series — deleted 120 minutes after last use, and 8 hours after upload, whichever comes first.
  • Results — deleted 24 hours after the job finishes.
  • Job records — structure names, timing, byte and voxel counts, and outcome are kept as your usage history. The image data they refer to is already gone.
  • Account — your identifier from sign-in, your email, and your API keys as SHA-256 hashes. We never store a key in a form we could show you again.

3. What we never do

  • We do not use your images to train or evaluate models. Ever.
  • We do not sell data and we run no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not read your images. Access is scoped per account at every query.

4. Deleting things

You can list everything currently held for your account and delete any of it immediately, from the dashboard or with DELETE /v1/volumes/{id}. For account deletion, write to us and we will remove the account and its history.

5. Sub-processors

  • Cloudflare — TLS termination and edge delivery.
  • Stripe — subscription billing. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Our own hosting — the API, GPU worker, object storage and database run on infrastructure we operate directly, not on a public cloud.

6. Security

Everything in transit is HTTPS. Sign-in is OpenID Connect with PKCE; the API accepts either a signed token or a hashed workstation key, and a leaked key cannot mint more keys or open a billing portal. Uploads use short-lived pre-signed URLs scoped to a single object.

7. Your rights

We aim to honour access, correction, export and erasure requests under GDPR, LGPD and comparable regimes, and to be HIPAA-aware in how the service is built. VoxTell is not a covered entity and we do not sign BAAs at present — if your institution requires one, talk to us before uploading anything.
