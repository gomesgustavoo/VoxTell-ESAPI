1. What this service is

VoxTell is a text-prompted organ segmentation service for radiotherapy planning. It proposes contours. It is not a medical device, it has no regulatory clearance, and it must not be used as the sole basis for a clinical decision.

2. Your responsibility

You are the clinician. Every structure the service produces must be reviewed by a qualified professional before it is used for treatment planning or delivery. You are responsible for the clinical acceptability of every contour and for compliance with the rules that govern your practice.

3. Acceptable use

  • One account per organisation; mint a separate key per workstation.
  • Do not share credentials outside your organisation or resell access.
  • Do not attempt to extract model weights, and do not use the service to build a competing model.
  • Do not upload data you are not entitled to process.

4. Plans, trials and billing

  • Every new account gets a 14-day trial. It is free, needs no card, and simply stops allowing new segmentations when it ends. Nothing is deleted because a trial lapsed.
  • Paid plans are monthly, in USD, and cancel at the end of the period you already paid for. There are no refunds for partial months.
  • A job is one image series with up to 16 structures. Quotas count submissions within a calendar month and reset on the 1st. Cancelling a job does not refund its quota, because the GPU time was already committed.
  • Every plan includes access to DicomSegVR at the matching tier, for as long as the VoxTell subscription is active.
  • Card processing is handled by Stripe. Prices may change with at least 30 days' notice, and never mid-period.

5. Availability

The service runs on a single GPU shared across a fair-share queue. We do not offer an uptime SLA on any plan. Higher tiers get priority queue placement and more concurrency, not a guarantee. Plan your clinical workflow so that a queue delay or an outage is an inconvenience rather than a problem.
